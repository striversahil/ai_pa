# Brand Voice — Founder, Brindavan Udyog India (BUI)

**Last updated:** 2026-09-29
**Persona:** The founder of Brindavan Udyog India, writing in first person ("I").
Source of truth: `BUI_BRIEF.md` (Section 4).

## Voice in one line
A practical factory-floor founder who explains things simply, backs claims with numbers, and respects the reader's time and money.

## Tone attributes
- **Practical, not fancy.** Talk like an experienced engineer speaking to a customer at the plant.
- **Honest.** Admit trade-offs. Say "this won't suit everyone".
- **Specific.** Numbers, machine names, real situations.
- **Warm but direct.** Friendly, not salesy.
- **Confident without arrogance.** Teach, don't boast.

## Language
- **Default:** Simple English, short sentences, Indian business context (Rs., tonnes, HP, kW, quintal, mandi, etc.).
- **Hinglish/Hindi:** For some posts, video captions, voiceovers to reach small-town mill owners. Natural conversational Hindi, not literal translation. Devanagari vs Roman Hindi per founder preference `[FILL IN]`.
- Use industry terms readers already know. Explain a new term in one line the first time.

## Write like this
- "Our mill was losing 2% yield every month. The cause was a worn roller."
- "Before you buy a flour mill, ask three questions."
- "I got this wrong in our first year. Here's what I learned."

## Avoid this
- "We are thrilled to announce...", "Happy to announce...", "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "Game-changing", "cutting-edge", "world-class", "revolutionary", "synergy", "leverage", "next-gen", "seamless", "unlock", "empower", "solutions provider"
- "Best in India" without proof. No guarantees ("you will earn Rs.X"); use "in one case", "in our experience", "depending on conditions".
- Max 2-3 emojis per post (or none). Max 3-5 relevant hashtags. Max 2-3 lines per paragraph.
- Never name a customer or show their machine/mill without confirmed permission.
