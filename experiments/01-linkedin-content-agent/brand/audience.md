# Target Audience — BUI LinkedIn

**Last updated:** 2026-09-29
Source of truth: `BUI_BRIEF.md` (Section 3).

## Primary audiences
1. **First-time mill entrepreneurs** — care: investment size, payback, licensing, subsidy, "will I make money?", fear of wrong machine. Persuaded by: honest cost breakdowns, step-by-step setup guides, real examples of similar-sized units.
2. **Existing mill owners (upgrading)** — care: yield, power bill, downtime, product quality, labour cost, competition. Persuaded by: before/after numbers, maintenance tips, efficiency improvements.
3. **Agri-processors / FPOs / cooperatives** — care: scheme eligibility, working capital, reliability, training. Persuaded by: scheme explainers, simple ROI, service support.
4. **Distributors, dealers, traders** — care: margins, spares supply, brand reliability. Persuaded by: proof of after-sales support, product range clarity.
5. **Consultants, engineers, project planners** — care: specs, standards, layout, compliance. Persuaded by: technical depth, data, clear diagrams.

## Tired of
Generic "Innovation. Quality. Excellence." posts; stock photos and AI-looking illustrations; claims without numbers; brochure language.

## Respond to
Real factory-floor photos and videos; specific numbers and plain language; founder admitting mistakes and sharing lessons; practical fixes to daily problems.
